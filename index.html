        How the score works

        Every message starts at 25 points. Signals that scammers use add points; reassuring facts take points away. The score is always between 0 and 100.

        • 0–29 · Safe — nothing in the message looks like a known scam pattern.
        • 30–59 · Caution — something looks off. Verify through the official app or official phone number before acting.
        • 60–100 · Likely scam — several scam patterns at once. Do not click, pay, or share codes.

        Signals include shortened links, links to raw IP addresses, unsafe (http) links, links that pretend to be bKash / Nagad / Rocket, requests for money or OTPs, urgency, threats, lottery claims, job fees, and “parcel held” customs fees — in Bangla and English. The full list is in detector.mjs.
